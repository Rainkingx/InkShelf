# InkShelf 2.0 — Public Beta 13

- Spotlight series cards are now fully tappable, with touch feedback and a chevron. Unverified editions retain their review warning.
- Pull down on Discover to refresh Spotlights, New This Week, Trending and Recommended for You, then rebuild the curated collection, Hidden Gems and Critically Acclaimed previews from the refreshed data.
- Cached content is retained when source refreshes fail. Existing daily themes and Spotlight schedules remain unchanged.

## Updating

From Beta 12, use the app update notification or Menu > Settings > Check for updates. Back up your library before installing. The package identity and release signing key remain unchanged.

This is a prerelease. InkShelf is an independent reader based on TachiyomiJ2K. Third-party sources and services remain separate. Use lawful sources and respect rights holders.

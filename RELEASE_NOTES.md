# InkShelf 2.0 — Public Beta 12

- Throwback Thursday now uses a dedicated classic-comics catalogue populated through enabled sources after startup.
- Up to 60 eligible classic editions can be cached, improving variety and the chances of filling all three daily picks.
- The strict pre-2000 publication-year rule remains in place. Modern editions and unknown years are excluded.
- Cached classics remain available when searches fail, and paused or disabled sources are excluded.
- Discover improvements and all existing Spotlights remain unchanged.

## Updating

From Beta 11, use the app update notification or Menu > Settings > Check for updates. Back up your library before installing. The package identity and release signing key remain unchanged. Classic results may populate after Home opens; availability depends on enabled sources.

This is a prerelease. InkShelf is an independent reader based on TachiyomiJ2K. Third-party sources and services remain separate. Use lawful sources and respect rights holders.
